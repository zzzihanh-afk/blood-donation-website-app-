# LALBINDU — DEVELOPMENT ROADMAP & MILESTONES

## 🗓️ Phase Execution Plan

| Phase | Title | Scope & Deliverables | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **PHASE 1** | **Project & Ecosystem Foundation** | Monorepo setup, Next.js 15 app, Expo React Native app, Supabase migrations, foundational schemas, RBAC, Auth, Bangladesh location hierarchy. | 🔄 IN PROGRESS |
| **PHASE 2** | **Donor Platform & Availability Engine** | Donor onboarding, blood group verification, calendar-month availability engine, donation history, donation submission & verification. | 📋 PLANNED |
| **PHASE 3** | **Universal ID Cards & Achievements** | Role-based digital ID cards (12+ roles), SVG/Canvas rendering, cryptographic QR tokens, public verification route, achievement badge engine. | 📋 PLANNED |
| **PHASE 4** | **Emergency Coordination & Smart Matching**| Emergency request posting, geographical & blood compatibility matching, quiet hours, push/SMS alerts, donor response tracking. | 📋 PLANNED |
| **PHASE 5** | **Organization & Healthcare Ecosystem** | Hospital portal, blood bank records, clinical staff verification, volunteer club coordination, campaign & blood drive management. | 📋 PLANNED |
| **PHASE 6** | **Full Mobile Production App** | React Native Expo screens (Android & iOS), bottom navigation, camera QR scanner, native share sheet, offline draft safety, bilingual BN/EN switcher. | 📋 PLANNED |
| **PHASE 7** | **Multi-Tier Admin Hierarchy Suites** | Super Admin master suite, Division, District, Upazila, Thana, City Corp, and Ward administrator dashboards with jurisdiction-scoped analytics. | 📋 PLANNED |
| **PHASE 8** | **Production Hardening & Verification** | End-to-end testing, RLS boundary tests, performance audits, offline PWA, Play Store & App Store bundle configs. | 📋 PLANNED |
